# Farooq Garments Style

I want you to build a complete, responsive website for a local Pakistani ladies clothing and custom stitching brand called Farooq Garments.

Business overview
Brand name: Farooq Garments

Location: Karachi, Pakistan

Niche: Ladies clothing and custom stitching services

Focus: Casual, formal, bridal, wedding, party wear, lawn, silk, maxi dresses, tops & pants, modern neck styles, branded suit stitching, and custom stitching from pictures and design ideas.

We already have a company profile PDF with our main content. Here is the text we want the site to be based on (you can reuse and slightly shorten where needed for web):

Core copy (from our profile):

“Farooq Garments specializes in ladies clothing, custom stitching, and designer wear. We create beautiful outfits for casual, formal, bridal, party, and everyday occasions — with quality stitching, stylish designs, and a perfect fit for every customer.”

“Farooq Garments is a ladies clothing and stitching brand dedicated to style, quality, and comfort. We create elegant outfits for women with professional finishing and attention to detail.”

“Our team works on custom designs, branded suit stitching, and fashion-inspired dresses to match your style and occasion — turning a reference picture or a simple idea into a finished, wearable piece.”

“We believe in delivering beautiful clothing that feels as good as it looks.”

Pages to create
Please build a multi-page website with the following pages and routes:

Home (/)

About Us (/about)

Our Services (/services)

Shop / Products (/shop)

Our Work (Client Gallery) (/our-work)

Contact Us (/contact)

The site should be mobile-responsive and look like a modern fashion / boutique website with a clean, elegant theme. Think of a simple premium look (light background, burgundy / maroon accents) similar to a minimalist clothing brand.

Design requirements
Modern, minimal, fashion-boutique style.

Color palette: light background, rich maroon/burgundy accents (similar to the PDF design).

Clean typography, plenty of white space, clear headings.

Mobile-first responsive layout.

Technical requirements
Use a modern stack (for example: React + Tailwind CSS, or Next.js + Tailwind) – choose what Lovable supports best.

Separate components for:
Navigation / header

Footer

Hero sections

Highlight cards

Services list

Category grid

Gallery items (images/videos)

Make routing clear and simple:
/, /about, /services, /shop, /our-work, /contact

Keep paragraphs short (2–3 lines) and web-friendly, as this is not a long-form site.

Image strategy
For category cards and hero sections, you can assume we will use AI-generated fashion / ladies wear images later, so just add clean image placeholders with proper aspect ratios (e.g., 4:3 or 16:9).

On the “Our Work” page, design the layout to handle lower-quality, real client images and simple embedded videos.
